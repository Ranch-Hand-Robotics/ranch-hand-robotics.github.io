# Ox

The workhorse for bigger chores. Ox is an open-source, large outdoor robot concept for hauling heavier materials and using powered attachments across farms, forests, and worksites.

Moving feed, shifting timber, preparing ground—the aim is a versatile platform for jobs that need more than a small carrier.

> **Status:** On the roadmap. The capabilities below are design goals, not confirmed specifications, and may change as development progresses.

## Design goals

- Remote-controlled and autonomous operation.
- Large payloads, such as feed, seed, or timber.
- Liquid payloads such as water or fertilizer, with broadcast or point spraying.
- A Category 2 three-point hitch for attachments such as tillers, cultivators, or buckets.
- A power take-off (PTO) for powered attachments or a hydraulic pump.
- GPS-RTK and optical positioning for route following and obstacle avoidance.
- Communication with other robots to share observations and coordinate tasks.
- Wheel or track options for different terrain.
- Emergency stop and remote monitoring alongside obstacle avoidance.

# Mule

Fewer trips, more done. Mule is an open-source, medium-duty robot concept for moving supplies and handling everyday work around a homestead, farm, field, or forest. Think smart wheelbarrow, with room for different payloads and attachments.

The aim is to let a robot handle the back-and-forth hauling while you focus on the job itself.

> **Status:** On the roadmap. The capabilities below are design goals, not confirmed specifications, and may change as development progresses.

## Design goals

- Remote-controlled, semi-autonomous, and autonomous operation with an easy-to-use monitoring interface.
- A target payload of 50 kg.
- Liquid payloads such as water or fertilizer, with broadcast or point spraying.
- A Category 0 hitch, with attachment compatibility to be explored during development.
- GPS-RTK and optical positioning for route following, with AI-assisted obstacle detection and decision-making.
- Communication with other robots to share observations and coordinate tasks.
- Remote monitoring and built-in diagnostics for maintenance and performance tracking.
- Single- or dual-arm mounting options for light lifting and maintenance tasks.
- Emergency stop and remote monitoring alongside obstacle avoidance.

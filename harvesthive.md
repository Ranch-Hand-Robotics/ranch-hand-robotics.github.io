# HarvestHive

More robots, less juggling. HarvestHive explores fleet coordination at the edge: managing resources and planning robot missions where a reliable cloud connection isn't a given.

> **Status:** On the roadmap. These are design goals, not confirmed specifications, and may change as development progresses.

## Design goals

- Coordinate robot fleets locally.
- Manage shared resources and plan missions.
- Work without depending on a cloud connection.

# Robots for ATAK

Put the team and its robots on the same map. Robots for ATAK is a planned plugin for coordinating robots and people from a phone or tablet during fieldwork, search and rescue, and disaster response.

> **Status:** On the roadmap. These are design goals, not confirmed specifications, and may change as development progresses.

## Design goals

- View robot telemetry alongside the team's map.
- Use waypoints to direct robot operations.
- Support peer-to-peer coordination with Decentralized Robotics and HarvestHive.
